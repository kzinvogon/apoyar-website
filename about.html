About the firm

Twenty-four years. Compact by design.

We began in 2002 building bespoke systems when commercial software ran out. In the twenty-four years since, we have run Magento at European scale, held peak trading weekends with millions of transactions, built and spun out our own products, and operated 24×7 support for systems that could not pause.

We are not an agency that has rebranded around AI. We are an engineering firm whose method has caught up with what was always true: that compact teams of senior people, given the right leverage, build better systems than large teams handed requirements through four layers of management.

How we work with AI

Several AI-assisted streams. One architect who signs.

Persistent, human-readable context lives in the repository: decisions, constraints, domain rules, interfaces, the reasoning behind each of them. That context is what lets several agents work in parallel without drifting apart, and what makes a new contributor — human or otherwise — useful within the hour.

Managing multiple agents is a discipline in itself: scoping their work so streams do not collide, keeping the context accurate, reviewing at the right altitude, and knowing which decisions never leave a human. It is the part we spent this year getting good at, and it is what we teach when clients ask us to install the method in their own teams.

Nothing reaches production because a model sounded confident. Every stream passes the same five gates.

Gate 01

Resolve intent

Architects turn business goals into decisions. Ambiguity is settled here in structured written context, not downstream in pull requests.

Gate 02

Contextualise

Each stream receives the architecture, rules, interfaces and invariants it needs — written down and version-controlled in the repository.

Gate 03

Build in parallel

Specification, implementation, tests and documentation run as concurrent streams, orchestrated and reviewed daily by lead architects.

Gate 04

Verify

Deterministic CI, automated test suites, security scans, and a named human architect's approval before anything can reach release.

Gate 05

Run & answer for it

Monitoring, telemetry, SLAs, out-of-hours incident cover and monthly board reporting — operated directly by the people who built it.
