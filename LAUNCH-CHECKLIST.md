# Apoyar.io launch checklist

Completed in this package:
- Responsive multi-page website
- White paper download
- Sustentus case study
- SEO metadata, sitemap, robots and 404
- Apoyar Architects logo, favicon and 1200x630 social preview
- Current UK company details in legal copy
- Privacy and Cookies pages
- Cookie/analytics preferences UI
- Consent-ready analytics hook (no tracker enabled by default)
- Contact form wired to architects@apoyar.io through FormSubmit
- Thank-you page
- Security/cache headers and redirects

One-time actions that require domain/account ownership:
1. Create or enable architects@apoyar.io.
2. Submit the live contact form once and approve FormSubmit's activation email.
3. Upload/deploy this folder to the apoyar.io hosting account and point DNS if changing host.
4. If analytics are wanted, create/select the analytics account and insert its production script in assets/consent.js.
5. Obtain legal review if formal legal sign-off is required.
