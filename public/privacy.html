2. What we collect

When you use our contact form or email us, we collect what you choose to send: your name, business email address, company, role, the type of engagement and timeframe you select, and your message.

When you visit the site, our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in server logs needed to deliver and secure the site. We measure overall traffic (page views, referring sites, popular pages, country and device type) with Plausible Analytics, provided by Plausible Insights OÜ (Estonia) with data stored in the EU. Plausible sets no cookies and stores no personal data: your IP address and browser details are used only to create a daily-rotating anonymous count and are never stored. Requests are routed through our own domain. We do not use advertising or social-media tracking.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

To reply to your enquiry and take steps you request before entering into a contract (UK and EU GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

To manage our business relationships, keep the site secure, prevent abuse such as spam, and understand in aggregate how the site is used (legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f)). We have considered these interests and believe they do not override your rights.

To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

4. Who we share it with

Netlify, Inc. hosts this website and receives contact-form submissions on our behalf. Plausible Insights OÜ processes anonymous traffic statistics. Our email provider stores the correspondence that follows. Some members of our delivery teams in the EU and India may see an enquiry when it concerns their work. We do not sell personal information or share it for marketing.

5. International transfers

Netlify is based in the United States, and our teams operate from the UK, EU and India. Where personal information leaves the UK or EEA we rely on an adequacy decision, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where the recipient is certified, or the standard contractual clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 24 months of our last contact. Where an enquiry leads to an engagement, records are kept for the life of the relationship and then for up to six years to meet legal and accounting requirements. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short period, typically no more than 30 days.

7. Your rights

Under UK and EU GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Write to sales@apoyar.eu; we respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, the data protection authority where you live or work, for example the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos in Spain.

8. Cookies

This site does not set cookies or use similar tracking technologies. See our cookie notice.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) throughout the site. Please do not send passwords, credentials or special-category information through the contact form.

10. Changes

We will update this notice when our services, providers or legal obligations change, and revise the date at the top.
