No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set any cookies, and does not use local storage, pixels, fingerprinting or other tracking technologies. There is no analytics, advertising or social-media tracking, so there is nothing to consent to and no cookie banner.

Fonts are served from our own site rather than a third-party font service, so your browser does not contact anyone other than us while you read these pages.

External links

Links to other sites, such as our LinkedIn page, take you to services with their own cookie and privacy policies. Those sites only set cookies once you visit them.

If this changes

If we ever introduce optional analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first, give you a way to change your choice at any time, and update this notice. Questions: architects@apoyar.io.
