ServiFlow

From monitoring noise to an AI-assisted Service Center — monitoring, service requests, SLA execution, development escalation and management reporting in one governed platform.

serviflow.app →

Managed-service organisations run across disconnected monitoring tools, email, ticketing, collaboration, development platforms and finance systems. Nobody has one operational picture, so SLA risk is invisible until it is a breach and management reporting is assembled by hand every month.

ServiFlow creates the common layer. Monitoring events become structured service work, customer requests are ingested and triaged, SLA clocks apply automatically, AI assists prioritisation and resolution, and the operational evidence consolidates into reporting.

The AI is governed rather than autonomous: it classifies, suggests, matches, detects SLA risk and surfaces patterns, while every operational action stays auditable and under human control. That constraint is the design, not a disclaimer.

What the platform does

Two-phase SLA engine

Response and resolve-after-pickup tracked separately, business-hours aware, with deterministic SLA selection. It is the piece that stops false breach reports and silent breaches alike.

Rule engine

Configurable routing, assignment and escalation with SLA-aware triggers. Every rule firing is auditable, so a breach post-mortem takes minutes rather than a day of log archaeology.

CMDB with assisted linking

Hardware, software, services and relationships tracked against work. AI assists the link between an incoming event and the affected CI, cutting triage time on recurring incidents.

Development escalation

Clean bi-directional bridges to Jira and GitHub. Support tickets become issues with context preserved; release notes flow back to close customer loops automatically.

Capacity planning

Ticket velocity, recurring issues and resolution effort converted into staffing models and queue health projections, so hiring conversations are backed by operational telemetry.

Consolidated monthly reporting

The end of three days spent assembling slides. Service levels, incident summaries, release velocity, recurring problems and commercial consumption gathered automatically into board-grade packs.
