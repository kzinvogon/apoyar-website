Interpretable Context Methodology

Apoyar's model draws on Interpretable Context Methodology (ICM): a methodology that treats structured filesystem organisation and human-readable context as an interpretable alternative to unnecessary orchestration complexity.

Instead of asking AI to rediscover the entire system on each task, relevant context is curated by stage: business intent, architecture, constraints, acceptance criteria and evidence.

Apoyar applies and extends ICM principles within a governed enterprise engineering model. Apoyar does not claim authorship of ICM.

Scope → Define → Build → Verify → Ship

Each stage has explicit inputs, outputs and approval conditions. AI can analyse, recommend, implement and verify. It cannot simply approve its own work or bypass designated human gates.

  • Scope settles business intent and boundaries.
  • Define turns intent into specifications and acceptance criteria.
  • Build executes within approved architecture and rules.
  • Verify supplies automated evidence.
  • Ship remains controlled and observable.

Context, not prompt cleverness

The durable advantage is not a single prompt. It is the quality of the knowledge representation used by people and AI.
