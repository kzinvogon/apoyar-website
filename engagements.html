Engagements

Six shapes of work. The first one takes days.

Every engagement below has a duration, a named architect, a written definition of done and a price agreed up front. We do not sell developer days, and we do not run discovery phases that quietly become the project.

Most clients start with a proof of concept or an architecture sprint. Both are cheap ways to find out whether we are the right firm, and both leave you with something useful either way.

01 · Proof of concept

See it working in days

A working proof of concept rather than a document about one: real data paths, real screens, deployed to a URL you can send to your board, your customers or your investment committee. Days, not weeks, because the method that builds platforms also builds evidence quickly.

For founders testing whether the idea holds, CTOs who need to show something before a budget round, and investors who would rather see a prototype than a proposal.

How it runs

A short brief, then two to five working days of build. You get a link, a walkthrough and an honest note on what would have to change to make it real.

What you get

A deployed prototype, the architecture sketch behind it, and a clear read on feasibility, effort and cost to production.

What we need from you

An hour to explain the domain, and a decision-maker who will look at it the day it lands.

Shape · Days, fixed fee from £5k
Team · One architect, one engineer

Where it runs

Scaffolded on lean modern infrastructure — Vercel, Railway, Netlify or Supabase — so a first version costs tens a month, not thousands, and no procurement is needed to see it.

02 · Architecture sprint

Decide what to build

A senior architect takes your intent apart — business goals, constraints, existing systems, the things nobody has written down — and returns a target architecture with a delivery plan and a cost envelope you can take to a board.

For CTOs and founders who know roughly what they want and need it made buildable, and for investors who want an independent read before committing capital.

How it runs

Week one: interviews and system review. Week two: architecture and options. Week three: plan, estimate and review. A short fourth week if the estate is complicated.

What you get

Target architecture, delivery plan, cost envelope, risk register, and the structured context pack an AI-assisted team needs to execute it.

What we need from you

Access to the people who know the domain, and whatever documentation exists. Roughly four hours of your team's time a week.

Shape · 3–4 weeks, fixed fee from £15k
Team · One senior architect

03 · Platform build

Ship the platform

An orchestrated pod takes resolved intent to production. Specification, implementation, tests and documentation run as parallel AI-assisted streams, each working from versioned context, with human review at every gate and CI from the first commit.

For product companies and founders who need a real system in the market this quarter rather than next year, without hiring a department to get it.

How it runs

Two-week increments, each ending in something deployed to an environment you can use. Weekly architecture review, daily orchestration, a demo every fortnight.

What you get

A live, monitored, documented system — with pipelines, tests, runbooks and handover material written as the work happens, not afterwards.

What we need from you

One empowered decision-maker on your side who can settle questions within a day. Delivery slows down when decisions queue.

Shape · 8–16 weeks to production, priced by phase from £45k
Team · Architect plus orchestrated pod

04 · Modernise or rescue

Take on what you inherited

Undocumented .NET, PHP, Magento or in-house applications that still run the business, often arriving with an acquisition or the departure of the person who built them. We map what is actually there, stabilise it, then modernise in increments.

For portfolio CTOs and PE operating partners holding software they did not commission, and for teams whose original developers have gone.

How it runs

A two-week assessment first: code, data, infrastructure, dependencies and real risk. Then phases in priority order, each with value at the end of it.

What you get

An honest assessment, immediate stabilisation, and a staged roadmap you can stop after any phase. No eighteen-month rewrite on faith.

What we need from you

Repository and environment access, and tolerance for hearing what the assessment actually finds.

Shape · 2-week assessment (£10k fixed), then staged phases
Team · Architect plus two engineers

05 · Managed run

Run it for us

Release pipelines, monitoring, out-of-hours response, tier-one resolution, capacity planning and consolidated monthly reporting — operated by the people who understand the architecture rather than a rotating service desk.

For teams whose systems have outgrown a part-time internal owner, and for anyone who has discovered what a peak trading weekend costs when it goes wrong.

How it runs

Onboarding and monitoring in place within four weeks. Then a steady cadence of releases, service reviews and a monthly report a board can read.

What you get

Agreed service levels, releases on a predictable rhythm, incident response with a named escalation path, and reporting that shows where time went.

What we need from you

Production access, an agreed escalation contact, and clarity about what genuinely counts as an emergency.

Shape · Monthly SLA from £4.5k/mo, rolling term
Team · Service lead, engineers on rota

06 · AI delivery transformation

Teach your team to work this way

We install the operating model inside your own engineering organisation: structured context in the repository, multi-agent orchestration, review gates, and the governance that lets auditors and boards accept AI in the pipeline.

For engineering leaders whose teams are using AI ad hoc and getting inconsistent results, and who would rather own the method than outsource the work.

How it runs

Six to twelve weeks embedded with your leads. We run the first streams together, then hand the wheel over while we are still in the room.

What you get

A documented delivery method, working context conventions, orchestration and review practices, and your people running it without us.

What we need from you

Two or three engineers with credibility inside the team, and permission to change how work is reviewed.

Shape · 6–12 weeks alongside your team, fixed fee
Team · Architect embedded with your leads
