The situation

Logistics software fails expensively and visibly. Orders do not flow, warehouses stall, brands lose a trading day they cannot get back. Bleckmann needed engineering that understood both the domain and the operational consequence — and support cover that did not depend on who happened to be awake.

The estate is a mix of .NET and PHP services built up over years, integrated with warehouse systems, carriers and client brands. It cannot be paused for a rewrite, and it has to absorb a load curve that goes near-vertical for one week a year.

What we own

Continuous integration and deployment, the release process, the .NET and PHP services behind it, DevOps and environments, monitoring, and a 24×7 support rota with tier-one resolution and a named escalation path. Nine years without a break in service, through staff changes on both sides.

Long engagements are their own discipline. Knowledge has to live in written context rather than in people's heads, or the ninth year becomes unmaintainable — which is exactly the practice that later made AI-assisted delivery straightforward for us.

Built for burst

Peak weekends are the test everything else is preparation for. Above one and a half million transactions in a single Black Friday, with capacity planned in advance, load paths rehearsed, and engineers on watch rather than on call.

Burst scale is not a feature you add in November. It is a set of decisions about queuing, idempotency, back-pressure and observability taken years earlier, and defended in every release since.

What changed with AI

The engagement now runs with AI assistance throughout — investigation, code, tests, documentation and change analysis — under the same review gates as before. Copilot and Claude sit inside the workflow; the architecture decisions, the release approvals and the accountability stay exactly where they were.

The practical effect is throughput: more change per month on a legacy estate, without a larger team and without a rise in incidents.

Nine years is the only claim that cannot be manufactured. Everything else about a supplier can be written well.
