Case study · ServiFlow

From monitoring noise to an AI-assisted Service Center.

ServiFlow was built by Apoyar to bring managed-service operations into one governed platform — monitoring events, customer service requests, SLA execution, development escalation, capacity insight and management reporting.

Built by Apoyar · developed in six months

One operational picture.

Managed-service organisations usually operate across disconnected monitoring tools, email, ticketing, collaboration systems, development platforms and finance tools. ServiFlow creates a common operational layer across those activities.

Monitoring events can become structured service work, customer requests are ingested and triaged, SLA clocks are applied, AI assists prioritisation and resolution, and operational evidence is consolidated into reporting.

AI that assists operations.

The product is designed around governed AI rather than uncontrolled autonomy: AI can help classify, suggest, match, detect SLA risk and surface patterns while operational actions remain auditable and controlled.

From raw activity to management intelligence.

ServiFlow's monthly reporting model brings together the operational story that is normally fragmented across several systems.

Software development

Capacity management
