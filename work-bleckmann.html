Case study · Bleckmann

Nine years of enterprise engineering. Now entering the AI-orchestrated era.

Apoyar has architected, developed, continuously delivered and supported business-critical logistics platforms for Bleckmann for almost a decade — combining enterprise architecture, CI/CD, DevOps and 24×7 operational responsibility.

Reverse logistics · Designed by Apoyar

Built for serious production scale.

Apoyar first architected and designed Bleckmann's reverse-logistics application around nine years ago. The platform has been continuously developed and supported since then, including peak trading periods exceeding one million transactions on Black Friday.

The application estate has evolved with the technology: .NET to .NET Core, PHP and Java/TypeScript front-end and integration components, and databases running on high-availability clusters.

Forward logistics · Five years later

Replacing expensive legacy software.

After several years of reverse-logistics development, Apoyar architected and implemented a forward-logistics label-printing application, replacing a high-cost incumbent platform and bringing the capability into a system Bleckmann could evolve with Apoyar.

The delivery model includes CI/CD, DevOps, production support and access to developers for 24×7 operational coverage.

The full engineering lifecycle.

Bleckmann demonstrates that Apoyar's current AI story is built on years of enterprise operational responsibility rather than experimentation.

Architecture

Business-critical logistics workflows designed for sustained enterprise use.

Continuous delivery

CI/CD and ongoing development across a mature application estate.

DevOps

Production operations, infrastructure responsibility and deployment discipline.

24×7 support

Operational coverage extending through DevOps and developer escalation when required.

The transition now

From proven engineering to AI-assisted engineering.

The Bleckmann estate is now benefiting from GitHub Copilot-assisted development. The next stage is to migrate the engineering operating model toward Apoyar's application of Interpretable Context Methodology.

The objective is not to rewrite proven systems for the sake of AI. It is to bring the productivity, structured context, parallelisation and governance advantages demonstrated in newer Apoyar platforms to a mature, business-critical estate.

The bridge between two eras

Then: enterprise architecture, CI/CD, DevOps and 24×7 support.

Now: Copilot-assisted engineering.

Next: structured context and ICM-based orchestration.
